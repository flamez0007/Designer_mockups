Meet Our Dentists

Since 2011, Willow Bend Dental Studio has been the #1 choice for families seeking high-quality dentistry in Houston. Our dentists — Dr. Maya Chen, Dr. James Okafor, Dr. Daniel Kim and Dr. Sofia Ramirez — combine advanced training with care that stands the test of time.

Dr. Maya Chen is a Board-Certified implant specialist, and all implant treatment is done in-house — no referrals, no runaround. Our friendly, multilingual team speaks English and Spanish, so every patient feels at home.
