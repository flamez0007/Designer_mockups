Good to know

Questions, answered

Still wondering something?

Our front desk is happy to talk it through — no pressure, no obligation.

Call (713) 869-9973
Do you accept my insurance?

We work with most major PPO plans, and Norma, our insurance coordinator, will verify your benefits before any treatment so you know exactly what’s covered. Bring your card to your first visit.

I’m nervous about the dentist. Can you help?

Absolutely — many of our patients came to us because of dental anxiety. Tell us at booking and we’ll build extra comfort into your appointment. We take everything at your pace, and never proceed without your okay.

Do you see children?

Yes — we’re a family practice and welcome patients of all ages. Booking the whole family on the same day is easy — just let us know the ages when you schedule.

What if I have a dental emergency?

Call us at (713) 869-9973 and we’ll get you in the same day — we hold slots open daily for urgent problems like a knocked-out tooth, severe pain, or a lost crown. If you’re in pain right now, phone us first; step-by-step first-aid guidance is on the way while you head over.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Your insurance card and ID, plus a list of any medications you’re taking. New-patient forms are sent ahead of time so you can skip the clipboard.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes — Norma, our insurance coordinator, helps you explore your options and find the solution that fits your budget. We’ll always share clear costs before treatment begins.

How often should I come in?

For most healthy adults, a checkup and cleaning every six months keeps problems small and catches issues early. If you have gum disease, a history of decay, or certain health conditions, we may recommend visits every three to four months. We’ll tailor a schedule to you — and send friendly reminders.

Are you accepting new patients?

We are — and we usually have same-week openings. New patients get a complete exam, X-rays, and a cleaning. Book online in under a minute or call us, and our front desk will confirm your time within one business hour.
