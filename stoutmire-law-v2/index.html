Common questions

Answers before you ever call

Straightforward information about fees, timelines, and what to expect from working with us.

How much does a consultation cost?

Your initial consultation is completely free and confidential. We will review the facts of your situation, explain the legal options available to you, and give you an honest assessment of your case — with no obligation to hire us afterward.

What does the free consultation cover?

We discuss your situation, explain your options, and give you an honest assessment — free and confidential, with no obligation.

How long will my case take?

It depends on the matter. Some cases resolve in a few months; others that go to trial can take a year or more. At your consultation we will give you a realistic timeline for your specific situation and keep you updated throughout.

Will I work with an actual attorney or a paralegal?

You work directly with Attorney Hawthorne. She leads your case and remains your point of contact from the first meeting through resolution. Support staff assist behind the scenes, but the lawyer you meet is the lawyer handling and arguing your case.

What should I bring to my first meeting?

Bring anything relevant to your matter: court documents, correspondence, photographs, and any paperwork you already have. Do not worry if you do not have everything — we can help you obtain records later. The most important thing is simply to reach out and start the conversation.

Do you handle cases outside Atlanta?

Yes — Attorney Hawthorne represents clients throughout Atlanta and the greater metro area.

Is everything I tell you confidential?

Yes. Everything you share during a consultation is protected and kept in strict confidence, whether or not you decide to hire us. We take client privacy seriously and never discuss your matter with anyone outside the firm without your consent.
